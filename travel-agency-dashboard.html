Standard STACS refund framework applies: funds are held in the client protection process. My Clients

Every candidate who selects one of your approved packages appears here as a client card. From a card you can view the candidate by their STACS reference, see their current stage and next target, manage the case, and message the candidate inside STACS.
No clients yet. A client card appears here once a candidate selects one of your approved packages and signs the agreement. Each card will let you view and manage the case, message the candidate and read their replies, and will show the stage that the candidate also sees when they sign in and that drives their Telegram updates.
What each client card will show
Candidate STACS reference (no personal contact details) · Selected package · Current stage and next target · Message thread (all messaging stays inside STACS) · Documents requested and received · Key dates. Sharing or requesting direct contact details is blocked, to protect both sides.

Progress & Status

Once candidates select your packages, each case appears here with its current stage and the next target, so you always know what to push next.
No active candidate cases yet. When a candidate selects one of your approved packages and signs the agreement, their case will show here.
The stages a case moves through
Package selected → Agreement signed → Deposit received → Orientation completed → Documents submitted → Documents verified → Employer documents submitted → Embassy application → Biometrics → Medical → Decision pending → Visa approved → Balance paid → Flight scheduled → Travel completed → Case closed.
