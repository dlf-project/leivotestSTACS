Not a recruitment agency

The trust layer between talent and the world

STACS doesn't replace employers, agencies or lawyers. It verifies people and documents, prepares them to global standards, and coordinates the journey — so every party can trust exactly what they see.

01

Verify

Identity, credentials and experience are checked and evidenced, slot by slot — verification is the trust engine of everything.

02

Prepare

A global-standard profile, a contact-free CV, and a mandatory orientation that readies people to work and live abroad.

03

Connect

Approved, prepared candidates are discovered by verified employers under clear, controlled, witnessed channels.

Why STACS

Built on trust, not promises

Four commitments that make STACS safe to rely on — for workers, employers and governments alike.

🔍

Verification is the trust engine

Every credential is checked and evidenced. Employers see proof, not claims — and verified talent rises faster.

🔒

Privacy by default

Profiles are invisible until approved. CVs are contact-stripped, and any contact happens only through witnessed, recorded channels.

🎓

Readiness, not just arrival

A mandatory orientation prepares people to work, live and thrive abroad — issuing a Readiness Certificate employers can see.

💭

Honest by design

STACS never guarantees a job or a visa — those decisions rest with employers and governments. We improve your odds, transparently.
