Become a STACS Verifier

Assessors & verifiers are the trust engine of STACS. You confirm candidates' credentials against real issuing bodies — and every confirmation you log is kept as legal-grade proof.
Evidence is mandatory. Every verification you submit must be backed by a confirmatory document from the establishment, agency, organization or authority that issued or can confirm the credential — with its reference — plus your remarks. These records may be checked, and anyone may contact the issuing body over what was submitted.
Step 1 of 2 — open your account
We only need your details and a password to get started. Once you verify your email and sign in, you can complete the rest of your profile — accreditation, what you can verify, and your proof document — at your own pace, and submit it for approval only when you are ready.
